Graduate Program, Scholarships & Funding

Prospective graduate students should review the relevant UNB graduate program, scholarship, funding, tuition, and academic regulations before applying to HBIS Lab.

  • Graduate Program: Systems Engineering Graduate Program
  • Scholarships: UNB Graduate Scholarships
  • New Funding Programs: New Student Funding Programs
  • Graduate Regulations & Guidelines: UNB Graduate Manual
HBIS Graduate Financial Support:
  • MASc students: financial support of $12,000/year, guaranteed for two years.
  • PhD students: financial support of $20,000/year, guaranteed for three years.
International PhD students: Eligible international PhD students automatically receive the PhD International Subsidy under New Student Funding.
